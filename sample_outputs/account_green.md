## 3. Intervention recommendation

No retention intervention required. Redirect CS effort toward 
expansion in the following sequence.

**Renewal — close within 30 days:**
- The renewal conversation is open and the school is engaged. 
  The CSM should aim to close the 2026 contract in October — 
  before the December rush. An early close frees CS capacity 
  for higher-risk accounts in November and December and signals 
  confidence in the relationship.
- Prepare the renewal proposal with a 3-year term option. 
  An account of this health and size is a strong candidate 
  for a multi-year commitment, which reduces future renewal 
  risk and improves revenue predictability.

**Expansion — initiate the conversation in the same meeting:**
- The school currently covers grades K–8. They have expressed 
  interest in expanding to grades 9–12 (high school) in prior 
  conversations. October is the right moment to formalise this 
  discussion — the decision-maker is engaged, the relationship 
  is strong, and the budget window for 2026 is open.
- Frame the expansion as a natural continuation of the school's 
  results trajectory, not as an upsell. Use the platform 
  adoption and student outcome data from the existing grades 
  as the evidence base.

**Reference account:**
- Given the school's NPS of 78 and its visibility in the 
  São Paulo premium segment, request a reference conversation 
  with two prospective clients currently in the pipeline. 
  The CSM should make this request in the renewal meeting — 
  accounts are most willing to advocate when they are 
  actively renewing and feeling good about the relationship.

## 4. Escalation decision

**No — escalation not required.**

This account is performing above expectations across all dimensions. 
CS leadership visibility on this account should be limited to

term discussion, both of which can be handled by the CSM 
with standard manager support.

## 5. Watch list

**Event 1 — Renewal close by end of October**
If the renewal has not been formally signed by 31 October, 
re-run the signal assessment. An unexplained delay in closing 
a Green account is itself a signal worth investigating — 
it may indicate an undisclosed concern or a competing 
conversation the CSM is not aware of.

**Event 2 — Expansion proposal response (November)**
If the school declines the expansion conversation or defers 
it to "next year" without a clear reason, note this in the 
CRM and re-assess at year-end. A school that closes the 
renewal but resists expansion may be signalling 
budget constraints not yet visible in other signals.
