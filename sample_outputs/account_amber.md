## 1. Risk tier

**🟡 AMBER — Score: 26/72**

This account presents a moderate, manageable risk profile. No single 
signal is at critical level, but three signals are active simultaneously 
— NPS decline, a recent pedagogical director change, and a renewal 
conversation that has not yet been initiated. The combination in a 
mid-campaign context (August) gives sufficient runway for a structured 
CSM-led recovery, provided action is taken in the next 7–10 days. 
Left unaddressed through September, this account would likely cross 
into Red territory.

## 2. Primary risk drivers

**Signal 2 — NPS trajectory (score: 2, weight: 3 — contribution: 6/9 pts)**
NPS dropped from 68 to 51 between year-end 2024 and mid-year 2025 — 
a 17-point decline that crosses into the Amber threshold. The decline 
is significant enough to indicate genuine dissatisfaction, not survey 
noise. The root cause has not been formally investigated by the CSM. 
Until the driver of this decline is identified and addressed, it 
represents an open wound in the relationship. A further decline at 
year-end measurement would push this account into Red regardless 
of other signals.

**Signal 8 — Key contact turnover (score: 2, weight: 3 — contribution: 6/9 pts)**
The pedagogical director changed six weeks ago. The new director has 
attended one introductory call with the CSM but the relationship is 
nascent. Critically, the new director was not involved in the original 
system selection — they inherited the contract. Directors who did not 
personally choose the system are statistically more likely to evaluate 
alternatives at renewal, particularly if they have prior experience 
with a competitor product.

**Signal 12 — Renewal conversation initiation (score: 1, weight: 3 — contribution: 3/9 pts)**
No renewal conversation has been initiated, though it is only August 
and the campaign has just opened. This is not yet critical but needs 
to move to score 0 within the next 4–6 weeks. The risk is that the 
NPS decline and director change create hesitation to initiate — 
the CSM may unconsciously delay the renewal conversation until 
"the relationship feels more stable." That delay is itself a risk.

## 3. Intervention recommendation

This is a CSM-led recovery with one specific escalation point.

**Within 7 days:**
- CSM schedules a dedicated NPS recovery conversation with the new 
  pedagogical director. The agenda is explicit: "We saw your NPS 
  decline this year and we want to understand what drove it and 
  what we can do differently in H2." Do not bundle this into a 
  routine check-in — treat it as a structured feedback session.
- In the same conversation, the CSM should begin building a 
  personal relationship with the new director: understand their 
  pedagogical priorities, their experience with learning systems, 
  and their vision for the school in 2026. This is relationship 
  investment, not a sales call.

**Within 14 days:**
- Based on the NPS conversation, identify 1–2 specific product or 
  service improvements the team can deliver before year-end. 
  Commit to them in writing. A director who sees a concrete 
  response to their feedback is significantly more likely to 
  renew than one who received a sympathetic conversation 
  and nothing changed.
- Open the renewal conversation — not as a formal negotiation, 
  but as a forward-looking discussion: "As we head into the 
  second half of the year, I'd like to start thinking together 
  about what 2026 looks like for the school." This framing 
  is non-threatening and positions the CSM as a partner 
  rather than a vendor chasing a signature.

**Escalation point — CS leadership involvement:**
- If the NPS recovery conversation reveals systemic product 
  dissatisfaction that the CSM cannot address independently, 
  escalate to CS leadership for a product team briefing and 
  a leadership-level commitment to resolution. This is a 
  conditional escalation — assess after the conversation.

## 4. Escalation decision

**Conditional — not required immediately, but prepare the trigger.**

This account does not require escalation today. The CSM has sufficient 
relationship and runway to lead the recovery. However, escalation 
should be pre-authorised and triggered immediately if: (a) the NPS 
recovery conversation reveals deeper dissatisfaction than the score 
suggests, or (b) the new director discloses competitor evaluation activity.

escalation.

immediately. A year-end NPS below 40 on this account would move it 
to Red regardless of recovery progress on other signals.
